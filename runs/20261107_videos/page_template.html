Rewind and Retry

GR00T N1.5 on its own versus GR00T supervised by our coordinator, on three long-horizon RoboCasa tasks (Kettle Boiling, Steam In Microwave, Pack Identical Lunches). GR00T is frozen; the coordinator only decides when to step in and what to do.

What the coordinator does
  1. The task sentence is split into steps (one per sub-task). After every chunk (16 actions), a tracker checks which steps are done.
  2. A step is stuck when it is not done after the time GR00T usually needs for it (the 90th percentile of its completions), or when a done step comes undone (e.g. an item falls out).
  3. Rewind: the robot plays its own recorded motion backwards, entry by entry at the forward speed (arm joints, gripper, base, torso), back to where the stuck step began. Then GR00T retries the step with fresh samples.
  4. Unstick: if the rewind is blocked by contact (e.g. the arm pressed against the inside of the fridge), a VLM (Qwen3.8-27B) looks at the cameras and writes one sentence in GR00T's own wording; GR00T follows it for 8 chunks, then the rewind resumes.
  5. The run ends at RoboCasa's own success check, or when no step has been finished for 4 of the current step's usual durations.

Whole episodes: GR00T's own successes plus, for each failed episode, the share of the 4 seeds our coordinator brought to success (no time limit). The 8 failed episodes that got stuck too late to step in count as failures. It assumes the coordinator leaves GR00T's own successes alone (in those, a slow step could also trigger a rewind; not measured yet).

Caveats. Step tracking and stuck detection here use the simulator's stage checks (an oracle); the VLM is used only for the unstick sentence. A VLM tracker is the next step. Without a time limit, all the extra successes came within 2× the official time (the median run used 1.4×); the time spent rewinding is not counted, and GR00T alone is kept at the official limit (when it fails, it is stuck, not out of time). Pack Identical Lunches has four pick-and-place steps; its stuck step often gets fixed, but the later steps rarely all get done.
